Technical Project Manager — AI & Software Products
Location: Kathmandu, Nepal / Remote
Employment Type: Full-time
Experience: 4–7 years
About the Role
We are looking for a Technical Project Manager to lead software and AI-enabled product initiatives from planning through delivery.
You will work closely with Product, Engineering, Design, QA, and business stakeholders to turn product requirements into executable delivery plans, manage dependencies and risks, and ensure teams consistently deliver against business goals.
This role is suited to someone who is comfortable operating between business requirements and technical execution.
Responsibilities
Own end-to-end delivery of software and AI-related projects.
Translate product requirements into clear milestones, deliverables, and execution plans.
Work with engineering teams to break initiatives into actionable work items.
Facilitate sprint planning, reviews, retrospectives, and project status reviews.
Track project scope, timeline, dependencies, risks, blockers, and resource requirements.
Partner with Product Managers and Engineering Leads to prioritize work and manage trade-offs.
Communicate project progress, risks, and decisions to technical and non-technical stakeholders.
Identify delivery bottlenecks and proactively resolve or escalate impediments.
Establish practical project processes and continuously improve team execution.
Use project data such as velocity, cycle time, delivery predictability, and defect trends to improve planning.
Coordinate QA, UAT, release readiness, and production launches.
Work with AI tools and automation to reduce repetitive project-management activities.
Maintain project documentation, decision logs, RAID logs, and delivery dashboards.
Required Qualifications
4+ years of experience in software project management, technical project management, or a similar role.
Experience delivering web, mobile, SaaS, or other software products.
Strong understanding of Agile/Scrum software development.
Experience working directly with software engineering teams.
Ability to understand technical concepts, APIs, databases, cloud infrastructure, and software development workflows.
Strong stakeholder-management and communication skills.
Experience with Jira, Confluence, or comparable project-management tools.
Demonstrated ability to manage multiple priorities and dependencies.
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Information Technology, or a related field.
Preferred Qualifications
Experience working on AI/ML products or integrating AI capabilities into software products.
Experience using AI tools to automate project-management or software-delivery workflows.
Scrum certification such as PSM, CSM, or equivalent.
Experience with AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud.
Familiarity with Git, CI/CD, APIs, and modern software development practices.
Experience working with distributed or international teams.
Product-management experience is a plus.
What Success Looks Like
Within the first six months, you will:
Establish predictable project execution and transparent reporting.
Improve visibility into delivery risks and dependencies.
Help engineering and product teams make better scope and prioritization decisions.
Reduce avoidable delivery delays and operational overhead.
Introduce practical AI-assisted workflows where they create measurable efficiency.
Build strong working relationships across Product, Engineering, QA, Design, and business teams.
Core Skills
Project Management: Agile, Scrum, roadmap execution, sprint planning, risk management, dependency management, stakeholder management
Technical: SDLC, APIs, databases, cloud, Git, CI/CD, software architecture fundamentals
Tools: Jira, Confluence, Slack, GitHub, project-management and AI productivity tools
Soft Skills: Communication, ownership, structured thinking, negotiation, problem solving, facilitation
