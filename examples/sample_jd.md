# Senior Technical Project Manager (AI & Platform Systems)
**Company**: Apex AI Systems  
**Location**: Remote / Hybrid  

### About The Role
We are seeking an experienced Senior Technical Project Manager to lead complex technical software initiatives across our platform and agentic AI systems. In this role, you will bridge the gap between engineering, product, and operations to deliver reliable high-throughput systems.

### Key Responsibilities
- Lead end-to-end agile sprint delivery and backlog prioritization for high-velocity software engineering squads.
- Drive cross-functional execution across distributed engineering teams, setting clear acceptance criteria and milestone deliverables.
- Implement automated workflows and AI-assisted tools (Claude, LLMs, Python automation) to streamline sprint planning and reduce manual operational overhead.
- Conduct systematic risk assessments and pre-mortem audits to eliminate delivery blockers before production releases.
- Establish measurable engineering performance metrics (cycle time, release frequency, defect density) and communicate progress to leadership.

### Requirements
- 4+ years of hands-on experience in technical project or program management for cloud or software platforms.
- Proven experience with agile methodologies (Scrum, Kanban, Jira backlog triage).
- Demonstrated ability to use AI automation tools (Claude, Python, API scripting) to optimize workflows.
- Strong track record of quantifying business impact (time saved, cycle velocity, bug reduction).
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or equivalent practical experience.
