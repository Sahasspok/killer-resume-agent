Privacy Policy for Killer Resume Agent

Effective Date: September 22, 2026

1. Single Purpose & Commitment to Privacy

Killer Resume Agent is designed with a single purpose: to help job seekers transform, calibrate, and optimize technical resumes into high-impact, ATS-certified career artifacts using the Google X-Y-Z formula and AI-assisted screening audits. We do not sell, monetize, or harvest your personal information, resumes, or job descriptions.

2. Information We Handle

  • Resume Data: Any text, PDF, or markdown document you upload or paste into the extension, including your name, contact information, employment history, and education.
  • Job Description Data: Job postings or requirements you paste or import from the active tab.
  • API Keys & Preferences: User-provided API keys (Google Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, Mistral) and local preferences.

3. How We Use and Store Your Data

  • Local Storage Only: Your settings, preferences, and custom achievements are stored strictly on your local device using standard browser local storage (localStorage).
  • No Developer Database or Tracking: We do not operate external databases, tracking servers, or analytics services that retain user resumes.
  • Direct API Processing: Data is transmitted directly to your selected AI provider (e.g. Google Gemini, Groq) or processed on your local backend server (localhost:5050).

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell or transfer user data to third parties outside of user-initiated requests to their chosen AI provider. We do not use user data for lending, creditworthiness, or advertising.

5. Contact

Questions or feedback? Open an issue on GitHub: https://github.com/Sahasspok/killer-resume-agent
